Placing and adjusting shapes

The shape library, dimensions by number and by handle, colour, rotating, tapering and twisting.

Every part in layerling starts with a shape. From them you build everything else: shapes complement each other, cut each other out or are joined into a new shape.

Adding a shape

Click «הוספת צורה» in the ribbon. The shape library opens.

The library is one list in sections, from «צורות בסיס» to «עזרים», each with the number of shapes in it. A click on a section's name folds it away, and the browser keeps what you folded; the first time, all of them are open. On top, «בשימוש לאחרונה» holds the last four shapes you put in. The search field finds a shape by its name and by other words for it, in English and German too: "USB" finds the «פתח מחבר». While you type, only the sections with a match show; an empty field brings your folded sections back as they were. «צורות מותאמות» on top is a single line until you keep a shape there.

The library closes after each shape you pick. Its title bar moves it, so you can put it where it does not cover the spot you are aiming at, over the properties panel for example; a double-click on the title bar puts it back under its button, and where you left it is kept in the browser. The pin in the title bar keeps the library open while you put several shapes one after the other. Its edges and corners pull its size, as wide and as high as your screen allows; the size is kept in the browser too, and a double-click on an edge sets it back. Pulled narrow, the library leaves out the names and shows only the pictures; the name appears when you point at a shape.

Pick a shape. It now hangs on the mouse pointer and lands where you click. Move it over a body and it lies down on the face under the pointer, sloped ones too, like Cruise in Tinkercad; over the empty plate it lands on the workplane. Esc cancels placing. A tool that waits for a click, such as the tape measure, the framing square, a note, «הצבת מישור עבודה», «השכבה על פאה» or the edge tools, is switched off when you pick a shape, so the click places the shape. The other way round, switching one of those tools on puts the shape down again. If you would rather have the shape appear in the middle of the plate, you can switch off placing by click in the settings (area «שליטה», switch «שיוט בהוספת צורות חדשות»).

On a face the shape snaps to the middle of that face when the pointer comes near it, as a corner or an edge snaps; hold Alt to set it down anywhere. Dragged from the library onto a face instead, it always lands in the middle of the face, or with Alt where you let go.

What the library offers:

  • «צורות בסיס»: «קופסה», «קופסה מעוגלת», «גליל», «קפסולה», «אליפסה», «מצולע» (three to twenty-four sides), «טריז», «גג», «כדור», «חרוט», «חרוט מעוקל» (its side bends inward into a trumpet like a pseudosphere or outward into a dome; «קוטר עליון» and «כיפוף» set it, and it can be hollowed out and filleted like any exact body), «פירמידה» (with three to twenty-four sides, so three-sided too), «גג מעוגל», «חצי כדור», «פרבולואיד» (a dome that narrows to a rounded tip, its height falling with the square of the distance from the axis) and «טורוס».
  • «כלים, צינורות ומעברים»: the «מעבר צורות» from one outline to another, say from square to round, the «צינור», the «צינור מכופף», made of up to twelve straight pieces with bends in between, and the «אגרטל».
  • «חורים ופתחים»: the «שקיעה» and «שקיעה קונית» for screw heads, the «חור טיפה» for horizontal holes and the «פתח מחבר» for USB, HDMI, DisplayPort and RJ45 sockets. The «תותבת הברגה» makes the hole for a heat-set insert in the body you selected; it is not dragged into the design.
  • «חיבורים»: «הברגה» (threaded rod, screw, nut and tapped hole), the «דסקית שקועה» under a countersunk screw, the print-in-place «ציר» and the «זנב יונה». More in Threads and mechanics.
  • «חלקי מארז»: the «עמוד בורג» for heat-set inserts and self-tapping screws, the «תפס נעילה» with its catch and the «צלע» or gusset. More in Threads and mechanics.
  • «מכניקה וחלקים נעים»: «קפיץ», «גלגל שיניים», the «מצמד Hirth» and the «גלגלת GT2».
  • «מערכות ותבניות»: «Gridfinity» bins and baseplates, the «לוח SBC», a single-board computer true to size to build a case around, and the «Fan», radial blowers and axial fans whose screw holes and air openings can reach through a wall.
  • «כיתוב ושלטים»: «טקסט», also along a circular arc, the «תג שם», the «תג סמל» and the «קוד QR». More in Text.
  • «קווי מתאר דקורטיביים»: «כוכב», «לב», «סהר» and the «חירוץ» for grips.
  • «עזרים»: the «סרגל», which is only a measuring tool and never shows up in an export.

You get a triangle in two ways: the «מצולע» with three sides is an isosceles triangle, the "Roof" of Tinkercad, and the «טריז» is a right triangle. Both are true triangular prisms. For a triangular hole, copy the shape, make the copy smaller, mark it as a hole and line it up with «מירכוז במישור העבודה» or «יישור». Both work with the shape's bounding box, the smallest box that encloses it, so a halved square still counts as a whole square. The command search (Ctrl+K) also finds both shapes under "triangle" or "roof". The wedge's properties also show «זווית שיפוע», the angle of its slope to the plate. Type an angle and the height changes to match at the same width, which is handy for setting a face workplane at an angle.

The «גג» in the library is that polygon with three sides, put in already lying on one flat side like a roof, with the corner on top. The handle on top changes the height of the triangle while its base stays on the plate, the handles at the bottom its width and length. Its three sides stay as they are; for another number of sides take the «מצולע».

The «קפסולה» can be narrower at one end than at the other, say for a guard over a belt between two pulleys. Under «מאפיינים» set «קצה קטן (קוטר)»; the large end is as wide as the capsule, and the sides run straight from one arc to the other. «מרחק מרכזים» is the distance between the two arcs' centres, that is how far apart the pulleys are. Change the small end and the centre distance stays, the capsule getting longer or shorter. The tapered capsule stays an exact body too, for fillets and STEP.

The «גזרה» is a disc that you cut back with the «זווית» (1 to 360°) like a clock hand, from twelve o'clock clockwise. The «קוטר פנימי» turns it into a ring or a ring segment (at 0 the piece stays solid), and «עיגול הקצה» rounds the two ends up to a half circle: that gives a curved slot, for example for monitor mounts and other parts that turn and get clamped. You set the diameter and the height as on the cylinder. The frame stays the full circle, so the shape turns about the circle's middle.

The «מצולע» has the «עיגול פינות» slider under «צלעות» and a drag dot on the model, like the «קופסה מעוגלת». One radius applies to every corner, for any number of sides. It is given in millimetres, so a stretched polygon keeps round corners; the largest value is where neighbouring roundings meet, and 0 is the plain polygon.

«צלע שטוחה מלפנים» turns the polygon by half a side, so that a flat side faces the front instead of a corner. Length and width are measured over the frame round the polygon: an octagon normally touches it with four corners, so both read from corner to corner; with the switch on it touches it with four flat sides and both read from flat to flat, the measure a wrench has. A hexagon then has its flat sides in front and behind instead of left and right. Turning the switch keeps the body as it is and changes the two numbers with it.

The shape's settings

As soon as a shape is selected, its settings appear on the right. At the top is the name; the pencil beside it («שינוי שם») lets you type a new one. Enter keeps it, Esc cancels, and an empty name brings back the shape's default name. Further right, the padlock locks the shape against accidental moving and the eye hides it. The arrow on the far left folds the settings down to their title bar. The cards below fold open and shut with a click on their title («מאפיינים», «מיקום», «סיבוב», «התחדדות», «פיתול והטיה» and the cards of the special shapes); layerling remembers which ones you left open, for the next shape and the next visit.

The settings sit docked at the right edge. If they cover something you want to see, drag them by their title bar and they float wherever you drop them; they stay there for the next shape too. Drop them back at the top right, or double-click the title bar, and they dock again. While they float or are folded down, the snap step moves from the bottom of the settings to the bottom right of the workplane.

  • «מלא» or «חור»: A solid stays, a hole takes material away. A click on «מלא» opens the colours; colours of your own that you mix there wait under «בשימוש לאחרונה» afterwards, the last eight. More in Solids and holes. The pin in the row «שמורים» keeps the colour you have chosen for good: it stands in a row of its own, newer colours never push it out, and the same pin lets go of it again; up to 21 colours are kept in this browser.
  • «שקוף»: Lets you see through the body, for example to spot a shape behind it.
  • «רב-צבעי»: For a group or a bundle; Shift+M switches it. Switched on, every part of the group shows in its own colour, also in a group that was cut with holes into one body (the walls a hole leaves take the colour of the body they were cut into). Picking a colour for the group switches it off and colours the whole group; the parts keep their own colours underneath, so switching it on again brings them back. A bundle has it switched on to begin with, and when it is switched off the bundle shows and exports in one colour. A group of solids only that you make starts with it switched off and shows as one body; groups made before keep showing their parts' colours. Once you switch it on for a group, the groups you make after that start with it switched on too, until you switch it off for a group again; the browser remembers this. In a 3MF export every part keeps its colour, so the slicer can map the parts onto filaments; OBJ carries the colours on its points, so the border between two colours is a little rougher there. Under the switch, «צבעי החלקים» lists the solid parts of the group or bundle (up to 12; holes are left out, and a group cut into one mesh has none), each with a colour swatch: one pick recolours that part alone, without opening the group, and is one undo step.
  • «מאפיינים»: The dimensions and everything that belongs to this shape. For a cylinder the diameter, the height and the number of sides. For a round tube or ring the «קוטר חיצוני», the «קוטר פנימי» (for a bushing on a shaft of a known size) and the wall thickness: any two give the third, in whatever order you type them. The outer diameter keeps the bore and changes the wall, the inner diameter keeps the outer one and changes the wall, and a bore wider than the tube makes the outer diameter grow by the wall. The tube's length is its height. A tube that is not round, after you pulled one of its sides, has the sides Length and Width instead of the one outer diameter; there the wall stays when you change the width. For a gear the teeth, for a spring the turns. A value you have moved away from its default gets a small arrow next to its name: a click takes just that value back, whatever else you did since, unlike Undo. A double click on a slider does the same, here and for the sliders in the workspace settings. Dragged with the mouse, the slider of a length snaps to the snap step, so a height comes out in whole millimetres (not with the grid off, and not on short sliders); a typed value and the arrow keys on the slider stay exact. «שמירה כברירת מחדל» at the bottom makes the current values the start values of this kind of shape (the same as «ברירות מחדל לצורות» in the settings), and «איפוס ברירות מחדל» goes back to the app's own. With «הקדח חותך כחור בקיבוץ» the bore counts as a hole once the tube is grouped: it then also cuts the wall of what the tube is grouped with, so two tubes in a T-junction open into each other. A bent tube with an opening has the same switch.
  • «הפיכה לאליפסה»: Only on a cylinder. A cylinder is always round: it has one diameter, and every handle changes both sides together. For an oval, click this and it becomes an «אליפסה» - the same body, whose length and width you then set one by one. A cylinder with a chamfer, a fillet or a wall, or a turned or grouped one, cannot be changed this way; take the «אליפסה» from the start for those.
  • «התחדדות»: Different sizes at the top and bottom, for example for a slope or a funnel. With «שמירת פרופורציות» switched on, Length and Width of the same end change together in the ratio they have, so a square top stays square; top and bottom stay separate.
  • «פיתול והטיה»: Twists the top against the bottom or shifts it sideways. That gives twisted columns and leaning towers.
  • «שיפוע לאורך»: Runs the top down towards one end. «הקצה הנמוך» picks the end, «גובה הקצה» or «שיפוע (°)» how low it goes, and «עקומה (%)» whether the top runs straight (0), bulges up (above 0) or sags (below 0). The other end keeps the body's height. Once it is set, an orange dot sits on the top of the lower end; drag it up and down like the height. That gives a roof whose height runs down along its length, a ramp or a slanted pipe end.
  • «היצרות לאורך»: Seen from above, runs both long sides in evenly towards one end, so that end stays in the middle. «הקצה הצר» picks the end, «רוחב הקצה» its width (0 makes a point), «מתחיל ב- (%)» where along the length the narrowing begins (0 % at the wide end, 50 % in the middle), and «עקומה (%)» whether the sides run straight (0), bulge out like a boat's bow (above 0) or are drawn in (below 0). Its orange dot sits on the side of the narrow end and moves across. Together with «שיפוע לאורך» it makes a bow or a ridge that runs down and in at once.

Taper and twist work on almost every shape. Only the gear, thread, spring, Gridfinity, hinge, pyramid, bent tube, teardrop, port opening, the screw-head cutters and the ruler leave them out: they have fixed measures of their own or, like the pyramid, a top of their own already.

«שמירה כברירת מחדל» keeps what you changed on this shape as the start of every new shape of its kind: the sizes, the settings of the shape and its colour. Colour a cylinder green, press the button, and every cylinder you add from then on is green; the other kinds keep their own colours. «איפוס ברירות מחדל» takes the kind back to what the app starts it with. The kinds that have defaults of their own are marked in the settings under «ברירות מחדל לצורות». A new design that you start from the start page begins with them too; the values are kept in this browser.

A tapered or leaning box, cylinder, ellipse, polygon, tube or ring keeps its exact shape, and so do the capsule, star, heart, crescent, dovetail a rounded box without rounded top and bottom edges, and a straight or round knurl (its chamfered ends narrow with it): chamfers and fillets work on it as on the plain shape, and the STEP export writes it. The same holds for a twisted shape: its section turns exactly as it rises, and the sides wind evenly from bottom to top.

«שיפוע לאורך» is offered on the box, rounded box, cylinder, ellipse, polygon, tube, ring, wedge and round roof. The wedge and the round roof lie along their depth, so they slope to the front or the back only. The sloped body keeps its exact shape, so chamfers, fillets and the STEP export work on it as on the plain shape; only a rounded box with rounded top and bottom edges, and a slope together with a taper, a twist or a lean, stay on the display mesh.

«היצרות לאורך» is offered on the same shapes except the tube and the ring, whose wall would get thinner across than along. A narrowed box, and a wedge or round roof narrowing, sloping or both, keep their exact shape in the same way. For now the display mesh only covers the rest: a narrowed cylinder, ellipse, polygon or rounded box, and a box that slopes and narrows at once. Chamfers, fillets and the STEP export then work on that mesh rather than on an exact body.

Typing is more exact than dragging. All number fields take millimetres, but also percentages: type "50 %" into a width of 40 mm and you get 20 mm. They calculate too: "15*3", "120-2*4" or "(40+2)/2" give 45, 112 and 21; x works instead of *. The field remembers the calculation, in the properties panel as well as in the dimension boxes around the selected body: click into it again later and the formula stands there instead of the number, as long as the value still matches it, and you change it in place, "(140+2)/2" becoming "(40+6)/2". Drag the body by a handle or scale it and the formula no longer fits, so the field shows just the number again. In a number field you have clicked, the mouse wheel and the keys ↑ and ↓ change the value by one step, with Shift a larger step and with Alt a finer one. In a size or position field Shift steps by the snap grid instead, and by ten steps when the grid is off. It also works without clicking into a field: hold the mouse button down on the name of a value, say "Width", and drag left or right; the value changes as you drag, with the same keys as the wheel.

Choosing colours

A click on «מלא» in the properties of a body opens the colour card, headed «צבע הצורה». On top it shows the hex value of the colour the body has now. Click it to type or paste a colour code such as #5EBDDB (5ebddb and the short #5bd work too) and press Enter: the body takes that colour, and with several bodies selected all of them do. Esc keeps the old colour. Under it are the ready colours of the palette; a click on one colours the body and closes the card.

«מותאם» opens the colour picker of your browser, for a colour of your own. What it offers depends on the browser: in Chrome and Edge it has a colour field, a slider for the hue, an eyedropper that takes a colour from anywhere on the screen, and the value as HEX, RGB or HSL, which you switch with the arrows beside it. The colour counts when you close the picker or click outside it.

A colour of your own that you mix there waits under «בשימוש לאחרונה» afterwards, the last eight. The pin in the row «שמורים» keeps the colour you have chosen for good: it stands in a row of its own, newer colours never push it out, and the same pin lets go of it again; up to 21 colours are kept in this browser. The pin is for colours of your own, the ones of the palette are always there. With several colours selected, or with a hole, there is no single colour to keep.

The colours of the work area in the settings have a simpler picker of their own, with a colour field, a row of ready colours and a field for the hex value; see The settings.

How round is round? The side count

Round shapes such as cylinder, cone, tube, ellipse or the bores have the switch «הצלעות לפי הגודל» and the slider «צלעות» in their properties. The «טורוס» has two sliders: «צלעות הטבעת» around the ring and «צעדי הצינור» around the tube. They follow the size there too until you hold them, so a large torus shows no facets. The reason: for the view and for STL, 3MF and OBJ, layerling draws a circle as a polygon of many small sides. The more sides, the smoother it is, and the more triangles it takes.

The «עובי הצינור» sets how thick the tube is seen from above. The height only squashes or stretches the tube, so a flat ring keeps its hole; at half the outer size the hole closes. Dragging the handles scales the tube along. Only a torus whose tube is as thick as the torus is high stays an exact body for chamfers, fillets and STEP.

Where the side count matters

  • In the export for the slicer (STL, 3MF, OBJ). A cylinder with few sides arrives there as a polygon.
  • When bodies and holes are combined. A bore cut by a hole with few sides is angular in the finished part. That includes screw holes.
  • In the view. Many very fine shapes slow the editor down.

Where it does not matter

  • For the STEP export. It writes the round shape, not the polygon.
  • For chamfers and fillets («שיפוע», «עיגול פינה») as long as the shape stays round. That is the case when you have not set a side count, when the count is at least the shape's default, or when the polygon strays at most 0.05 mm from the circle. The default is 96 sides for cylinder, ellipse, tube and cone, 64 for the «גג מעוגל», 24 steps for the «כדור», 32 for the «חצי כדור» and the «פרבולואיד», 96 sides and 24 steps for the «טורוס», and a «איכות» of 32 for a round «צינור מכופף».
  • For a thread, a spring or a helical or herringbone gear. The edge tool and the STEP export take its exact body; its «איכות» only sets how finely it is drawn.

Where it matters again

  • For chamfers and fillets on a shape with few sides. If you set the side count below the default, the edge tool takes the shape as it is drawn: a cylinder with 6 sides stays a hexagonal bar, and the fillet runs round its six edges. That is how you can build, for instance, a nut pocket with rounded corners. For a round shape, leave «הצלעות לפי הגודל» on.

How to use it

  • Leave «הצלעות לפי הגודל» on. Then layerling picks the side count from the diameter so that the polygon strays at most 0.005 mm from the true circle, far below what a printer resolves. Small shapes get at least 24 sides, large ones more.
  • Fewer sides you take when you want a polygon on purpose (but there is the «מצולע» for that) or when the editor gets slow with very many round shapes.
  • More sides you hardly ever need. Only on very large round parts, when you see edges in the STL.
  • For parts you only treat with edges or hand on as STEP, the side count does not matter.

Working with the handles

Besides the settings there are handles on the shape itself:

  • The corners and edges make the shape larger or smaller. Drag one past the opposite side and the shape keeps growing there, on the other side of that edge. The arrow on top does the same below the base. Hold Shift while dragging a corner to scale width, depth and height together and keep the proportions; hold Alt to scale from the center instead of the opposite corner. Without a keyboard, or if you do not want to hold a key, switch on «שמירת פרופורציות» at the top of the properties: then the corners always keep the proportions, and typing one of width, depth or height scales the other two by the same factor. The setting is remembered.
  • The blue arrow above the shape lifts or lowers it; with Alt held from the start, a copy is made where you let go, so you can stack. The handle on the top face changes the height. The handles that change one measure carry the colour of its axis, as the curved arrows do: the middle of a side (width red, depth green) and the top (height blue); the corners change two measures and stay white.
  • The red and the green arrows on top move the shape along X or along Y only. The shape then follows the mouse along that line, even when you look at the plate from low down, where a free drag runs off far. It snaps to other shapes as a drag does, and afterwards you can click the distance moved and type the exact value. If they get in the way, switch off «הצגת חיצי ההזזה האדום והירוק» in the settings.
  • The curved arrows rotate it.
  • The numbers beside the shape show the dimensions. A click on one opens a field in which you type the number you want. With Enter the shape grows from the edge opposite the number, as when you drag; with Alt+Enter it grows round its middle, so a shape that was aligned with another stays aligned. With «שמירת פרופורציות» ticked in the properties, or with Shift+Enter, the other two dimensions follow by the same factor. That holds for width and length; the height always grows upward from the underside. The fields in the properties on the right keep the middle without a key. There too, Shift+Enter makes the other two dimensions follow. Tab keeps the number and opens the next one (width, depth, height); Shift+Tab goes back. In a field of the properties that you have clicked, the mouse wheel changes the value as well: a unit a notch, ten times as much with Shift (in a size or position field the snap grid), a tenth with Ctrl and a hundredth with Alt. A field that you have not clicked leaves the wheel to the panel, which scrolls.
  • A click on a corner (without dragging) opens width and depth together. Type the first, press Tab for the second and Enter to apply both. Esc cancels.

Once a shape is turned by right angles, for instance laid on its side with «השכבה על פאה», the fields in the properties are named after the direction each measure runs in now: «גובה» is what goes up, «רוחב» runs along X and «אורך» along Y. A field with a name of its own, such as a countersink's head diameter, keeps it. Typing the height of a shape lying down therefore changes the vertical measure, and its underside stays on the plate. The handles of a shape lying on its side on the plate or on another level workplane stand upright as well: the one on top pulls it up, and those at the bottom change the measures that run along the workplane, whichever of the shape's own measures that is. A shape tilted at any other angle shows its own measures in the fields, and a note below says so. Under the note it also shows the overall size in space, so you can read how high a tilted shape really stands; it is for reading only.

Without the mouse, use the keyboard: the arrow keys move the selection by one snap step (0.01 mm when the grid is off), with Shift by five, or by ten if you set «קו רשת כהה יותר כל» to «10 צעדי רשת» in the settings under «מישור עבודה»: as far as the next darker line, as in Tinkercad. They follow the view: → moves to where the screen's right is, ↑ away from you, even after turning the plate. Push a shape out of the picture and the view moves along. Ctrl+↑ and Ctrl+↓ raise and lower it. A run of arrow presses is one step in the history: as long as the next press follows within a second and a half, Undo takes back the whole run, not a press at a time. R rotates by 45°, Shift+R by 22.5°. D drops the selection onto the workplane.

The handles pull a shape as large as your plate, at least 220 mm wide and deep. Typing a number in the properties goes beyond that, and a shape made that large does not shrink back when you grab it again. A ceiling of your own per shape is set in the settings under «ברירות מחדל לצורות» with «מגבלת גודל מותאמת».

The snap step is at the bottom right of the editor and can be changed at any time.

Copying and duplicating

«העתקה», «הדבקה» and «שכפול» are in the ribbon («לוח העתקה»). Ctrl+D duplicates a selection. layerling remembers how you last moved or rotated the copy and applies the same again on the next duplicate. That produces a row of holes or steps in a few key presses. It works with several parts at once too: duplicate a whole layer, lift the copy, and every further Ctrl+D stacks the next layer at the same distance. For regular arrangements there is also the pattern, described in Selecting and arranging. If you also resized the copy, every further copy grows by the same factor again, as in Tinkercad. A shape you gave a name of your own gets a copy that is called after it, "Holder (copy)", then "Holder (copy 2)" and so on, so the object list stays readable; a shape that only has the name layerling handed out ("Box") keeps it.

Tip: If a shape refuses to take the size you want, it is often the limits for new shapes. Under «ברירות מחדל לצורות» in the settings you decide how each shape starts and how large it may get.

Custom shapes

A holder, a grip or a base plate you need again and again goes into the «צורות מותאמות». They sit at the top of the shape library, above the «צורות בסיס», and fold away with their arrow.

  1. Select one or more bodies, holes, groups or an imported model included.
  2. Open the shape library and click «שמירת הבחירה». Enter a name and confirm with «שמירה».
  3. In any design, a click on the tile inserts the shape, like any other shape. You can also drag the tile onto the workplane; it then lands where you let go. The shape then hangs on the pointer, like any other: it follows it, also over the faces of other bodies, and a click sets it down; Esc puts it away.

Several bodies come back together, placed as they stood to each other, and stay selectable one by one. Notes pinned to the saved bodies go along and come back closed, pinned to the inserted ones; free notes and reference points stay in the design. The pencil on a tile renames it, the bin removes it after asking. Shapes already inserted into designs stay as they are.

The «צורות מותאמות» live in this browser, apart from the designs, and are there again the next time you start. «גיבוי הכול» on the start page backs them up with the designs, and opening the backup brings both back. To take only the shapes to another browser, «גיבוי צורות מותאמות» (the arrow pointing down) saves them as a ZIP file and «טעינת צורות» (the arrow pointing up) brings them back there. «טעינת צורות» also takes single .lyl designs; each one becomes a shape. Shapes already there are not added twice.

Typing in the search of the shape library finds your custom shapes by their name too, also those inside folders; they show above the library's own matches. The button with the arrow and the letters A to Z, beside the folder button, puts the custom shapes in order by name instead of by the day they were saved; a second click brings the old order back, and the browser remembers the choice.

Folders

Many custom shapes sort better into folders. «תיקייה חדשה» (the folder with the plus, next to «שמירת הבחירה») asks for a name and makes a folder at the top; the folder with the plus on a folder, «תיקייה חדשה בתוכה», makes one inside it. Folders go three levels deep: a folder on the third level holds shapes but no further folders. Folders sit above the shapes that are in none, in the order of their names, and open and close with their arrow; the number on a folder counts the shapes in it and in the folders inside it. When saving, choose the folder in the list next to the name; «ללא תיקייה» keeps the shape at the top.

The folder with the arrow on a tile moves that shape into another folder, or back out with «ללא תיקייה». The pencil on a folder renames it, the bin deletes it after asking, together with the folders and shapes in it. A folder itself cannot be moved: make the new one where it belongs and move the shapes over. A backup keeps the folders, and loading it puts the shapes back in the same place.

Custom shapes on the server

With the shared server store switched on, the section shows two groups: «בשרת» and «בדפדפן הזה». When saving you choose where the shape goes; the server is chosen to start with. There it lives in the folder Custom shapes, as a .lyl file of its own with a picture, ready on every device that reaches the server; its folders are folders inside Custom shapes. Each side has folders of its own, and a shape moves between the folders of its side. The cloud on a browser tile puts that shape on the server; it is then no longer kept twice.

The Custom shapes folder shows on the start page as well. Open a shape there like a design, change it, and it is saved automatically like any design on the server; the next insert takes the new version. As the server store has no login, everyone who reaches the server sees the same shapes.

Wrapping around a cylinder

Should a pattern, a logo or lettering go onto a cup, a can or a tube? First lay it flat on the plate, as an imported SVG or as text for example, the way it should look from above. Then wrap it around a cylinder in the shape's settings under «עטיפה סביב גליל»:

  1. Open «עטיפה סביב גליל» and enter the «קוטר» of the cylinder wall, such as the outside diameter of your cup. Tenths of a millimetre work too, such as 63.5.
  2. Click «עטיפה». What ran from left to right now runs around the cylinder, the back edge becomes the top, and the thickness stands out of the wall.
  3. Select the wrapped shape and the cylinder and centre both, left to right and front to back. The middle of the wrapped shape is the cylinder's axis, so it then sits right on its wall.

Every body can be wrapped, a shape from the library too: a round roof, say, that should sit on the wall of a round tower. Lay it on the plate the way it should look on the wall, its round top at the back.

For an engraving, switch on «פנימה (חריטה)»: the thickness then goes into the wall. Make the shape a hole, centre it and group it with the cylinder.

A shape that is wrapped already is a mesh of the wrapped thing and cannot be wrapped again: undo the wrapping, or wrap the flat original once more with another diameter.

The result is a mesh like an imported model. Undo lays the shape flat again; for another diameter, undo first and wrap again. It cannot go further than once around the cylinder; then layerling names the smallest diameter that fits.

A surface texture

Grooves, knurling, honeycomb or bumps on a body, for a better grip or for looks: open «מרקם משטח» in the shape's settings. The button «מרקם משטח» in the «משטח» group at the top does the same: it opens and closes the card for the selected body and stays lit while the card is open. Unlike the other cards it is not kept: when you choose another body, or none, it closes again.

  1. Choose the «דוגמה», its «מרווח» and its «עומק». «כיוון» says whether it goes into the body or stands out of it. For «בליטות», «צורת בליטה» chooses between «חצי כדור», «חרוט (מחודד)», «מעוגל ברכות» and «שטוח למעלה». Under «סידור» they sit «מדורג», every other row half a spacing along, or in «שורות ישרות».
  2. Under «מונח על», take «סביב ציר» for round bodies such as a cup, a knob or a handle. The pattern runs round the «ציר», and the spacing is rounded a little so that a whole number of grooves or cells goes round: the pattern closes without a seam. «מכל הצדדים» suits bodies that are not round, «שטוח, לאורך הציר» a lid or a plate seen from above. Leave it at «אוטומטי» and Layerling chooses for you: «סביב ציר» on round bodies and on angular ones standing upright such as a box - the pattern then follows the outline and runs round the edges without a seam, a pattern standing out bending round them -, «מכל הצדדים» on other angular ones.
  3. Click «החלת מרקם». A large body takes a few seconds; you can keep working meanwhile.

To give the pattern to only some faces, say only the top of a box, click «בחירת פאות» and then the faces in the view: the top, the bottom or a side. A face reaches up to the body's sharp edges; on a cylinder the mantle is one face, and the top and the bottom are one each. Picked faces light up, a second click takes one out again, and «שוב כל הפאות» gives the pattern the whole body again. Once picking is done, «הצגת הפאות שנבחרו» lights the picked faces of the shown layer again for a look, without changing them, which helps with several patterns on one body. Only the picked faces get the pattern; all others stay as they were and keep few triangles. On a top or a bottom the pattern lies flat; on a round top, grooves, rope and knitting run in rings round the middle, like a tree's year rings. «כיוון למעלה/למטה (°)» turns the pattern on the top and bottom: at 0° the rows run along the front edge, at 90° along the side edge. «למעלה ולמטה» switches grooves, rope and knitting there between rows and rings; on an angular body the rings follow its outline. «קצה למעלה ולמטה» says what happens at the edge between the side and a patterned top or bottom: «חד» lets both end at the edge. With «הצד עובר מעל» the side's pattern bends round the edge and takes the whole top: rows of rope, knitting and level grooves run on as rings, upright and slanted grooves as rays almost to the middle. «קיפול קצר» does the same as a rim only; inside lies the top's own pattern, as «למעלה ולמטה» sets it. «מסגרת» lays a border round the edge for every pattern – a cord, a cast-off chain or a round bead. A tight round (a fillet) ends a face as well: on a cup with rounded rims the outside, the rounds, the rim, the inside and the bottom are faces of their own, so a click on the outside takes only the outside. A wide round, about a quarter of the body or more, belongs to the face, as a sphere is one face. With picked faces, «דפנות חיצוניות» and «דפנות פנימיות» do not count.

Several patterns on one body – bricks round the sides, knurling on the top – are layers: «שכבה» beside the first adds one, and picking is switched on for it at once; click the faces it should get. Every further layer goes only on its own faces, the first on all the others. The tabs show one layer at a time with its settings, «הסרת השכבה הזו» takes one away. All layers are made together on the body under the texture, so changing one or taking the texture off works as for a single pattern. A text needs no faces as a layer: it goes where it is put.

To give the pattern to just a place on a face, say a round patch of knurling on a lid, click «ציור אזור», press on the face in the view and draw round the place with the button held. The pattern then goes only inside the outline you drew and the rest of the face stays smooth; if you press beside the body, the view turns as usual. «ציור אזור מחדש» draws it anew and «הסרת האזור» takes it away. Every layer can have a region of its own, also on the same face.

Under «עוד» you choose where the pattern goes: outer and inner walls, how far the bottom and the top stay smooth (a smooth bottom keeps the body flat on the bed), a band of the height, and whether sharp edges stay smooth. Next to everything that stays smooth, the pattern fades in gently; «דעיכה לאורך» sets over how many millimetres. The smaller it is, the closer the pattern comes to an edge; a pattern standing out runs out right up to it. On the flat sides of an angular body like a cube it ends at a straight border that far away instead: grooves and diamonds stop with a short wall, and only whole bumps stay. «פרופיל» shapes the cross-section of a groove or joint: pointed and flat come out exactly, round in as many steps as «פירוט» gives it.

Besides «חריצים», «חירוץ», «חלת דבש» and «בליטות» there are more patterns. «תאים» are irregular cells like paving stones or dried clay; their joints are cut cleanly as the honeycomb's are, and another «סידור» lays them out anew. «מרוקע» is round, overlapping dents like the hammer strikes on a hand-hammered copper mug, each a little different in size and depth; another «סידור» lays them out anew too. «סריגה», «חבל» and «קליעת סל» copy knitted stitches, a wound rope and a basket of stakes and weavers; they start raised and round, at the size of their models. «גרעין» roughens the surface finely, as if blasted; the grain lies in space, without a seam or stretching. «תמונה» takes a grey picture as a height map: one of the ready ones such as wood, topography or terrazzo, or your own with «טעינת תמונה משלך…». Light becomes the full depth; «רוחב אריח» sets how wide a tile is, and under «אריחים» the tiles repeat, mirror or are staggered. A dark drawing on a transparent ground, such as black lines in an SVG, counts by its lines: they become the full depth. With «פעם אחת» the image is pressed on a single time, like a label: «מסביב (°)» and «גובה (%)» place its middle as for a text, «רוחב התמונה» sets its width, and the surface beside it stays smooth. With faces picked, it always lands on them, even where «מסביב (°)» and «גובה (%)» aim past them. A drawing in two tones (an SVG, PNG or JPEG), pressed on once, is cut along its outlines as a text is, so slanted lines and curves come out clean and not in steps.

«קרבון (טוויל)» lays carbon fibre woven in twill onto the body: upright bands and crossing bands, over and under each other, one band on from band to band, so the familiar diagonal shows. «אריגה» chooses «טוויל 2/2», the even weave of most carbon parts, or «טוויל 2/1» and «טוויל 3/1», where the upright bands show long and the crossing ones short. «רוחב רצועה» sets how wide the upright bands are; «רוחב רצועה חוצה» makes the crossing ones wider or narrower for an uneven weave (0: as wide). Round a body the band width is rounded a little so the weave closes without a seam. The single fibres are far too fine for a print: the bands with their crossings are what shows.

«לבנים» lay a brick wall onto the body, with joints sunk in like mortar. «קשר» chooses how the bricks are laid: «קשר רץ» as most walls are, «קשר ערימה», or with short bricks between the long ones («קשר אנגלי», «קשר אנגלי מוצלב», «קשר פלמי»). «אורך לבנה» and «גובה נדבך» count from joint to joint and are rounded a little so that whole bricks fit: round a cup, from edge to edge of a box's side and from the bottom to the top; «רוחב מישק» says how wide the joints are. Under «עוד», «לבנים לא אחידות (%)» lets every brick lie a little deeper, as in an old wall.

«ריבועים» and «משולשים» lay tiles of one size in a grid onto the body, raised or pressed in, their walls cut steep and clean; the triangles stand in rows, pointing up and down in turn. «גודל ריבוע» or «גודל משולש» and «רווח» stay exactly as you set them – squares of 3.2 mm, 3.2 mm apart, are eight lines of a 0.4 mm nozzle each and print cleanly. The grid is centred on every face; round a round body the gap is rounded a little so that a whole number goes round. With «מהקצה התחתון» the first row starts right at the bottom edge and reaches down into the first layer, where a brim holds it. For tiles on only the lower half of two sides, pick the two faces and put a helper body over the upper half with «מרחיקה את המרקם» under «מסכת מרקם» (see below). Under «עוד», the «פרופיל» «מחודד» makes pyramids of them, «עגול» and «חצי עגול» cushions.

«בית קורות (Fachwerk)» and «קיר אבן» are made for model buildings, such as the houses of a model railway or a diorama. «בית קורות (Fachwerk)» raises beams out of the wall, the plaster between them lying lower: posts «רוחב לוח» apart, a sill and a plate to every storey of «גובה קומה», and «קורות אופקיות לקומה» across it. «חיזוק» chooses how much wood the panels hold: «אלכסונים» lean towards the middle from both ends, «צלבי X» sit under the lowest rail, «חיזוקי פינה» in the top corners, and «מעורב» puts braces at the ends with crosses and corner braces between, as on many old houses. «רוחב קורה» and «גובה קורה» make the beams wide and deep, «סיבי עץ (%)» gives them a grain: 0 % none, below 50 % more and finer lines, above it two wide, deep grooves – from planed wood to rough planks. As with bricks, panels and storeys are rounded a little so that whole ones fit, from the bottom edge up. «קיר אבן» lays an old wall of stones of different sizes in rough courses, the mortar joints lower and every stone bulging a little by its own amount. «עגלגלות (%)» goes from broken stone with sharp edges (0 %) to rounded field stones (100 %); «שונות גודל (%)», «רוחב מישק» and «סידור» say how different the stones are, how wide the joints and how they lie. Both are shape only, in one colour, so «דוגמה כחלק נפרד» can make the beams or the stones a part of their own in a second colour.

The pattern «סדקים (קינצוגי)» grows cracks across the surface as in kintsugi, with kinks, branches and a width that tapers towards the end. «התחלה» tells where they begin: at the top rim of a vase or a cup, at the bottom, from the rim to the middle of a plate, or anywhere; under «סוג» they become lightning. With «מילוי זהב כחלק נפרד» the notch is filled by a part of its own in gold, for a second filament; it goes along when you change or remove the texture. Under «עוד» you set branches, zigzag and chipped edges, and another «זרע» throws the cracks anew.

«קליפת עץ» lays a tree's bark round a log: «אלון», «אורן», «אשוח», «ליבנה» or «אשור», each at its own size and depth. «סיבי עץ» makes the body a piece of wood: under «עץ» choose oak, ash, beech, pine or walnut, under «ניסור» a log without its bark or a board, flat-sawn with its arches or quarter-sawn with straight lines. «רוחב טבעת» makes the grain fine or coarse. With «טבעות שנה בקצוות» the ends of a log show the annual rings, and they match the grain on its sides: the rings run through the whole body as in a real piece of wood. Under «עוד» you add «עיניים (קשרים)», and the rings bend round them.

With the pattern «טקסט (מודבק)» you stick a text onto the body like a label pressed on: the surface is unrolled round the place, so the letters keep their size on a vase's belly, on a slanted wall or over the edge of a box. "Around" (0° is the front, 90° the right) and "Height" in % along the axis say where it goes; with the axis on X or Y, 0° is the top - that puts the text on a flat plate. «כיוון» engraves it or raises it. Over a real corner it tears like a sticker; the status message says so then. «אותיות מלמעלה למטה» stacks the letters top to bottom here too. With faces picked, the text always lands on them, even where «מסביב (°)» and «גובה (%)» aim past them.

To keep part of the body smooth, or to texture only a part, use a helper body: put a box, a cylinder or a sphere where it should be, select it and choose under «מסכת מרקם» either «מרחיקה את המרקם» or «מרקם רק כאן». It turns transparent, and it counts when the other body gets its texture, also while it is hidden. Hide or delete it before printing.

The result is a mesh like an imported model, and it keeps the body it was made on. Select it later, also after saving, and the card shows the settings it was made with: change them and click «שינוי מרקם», and the texture is made again on that body, where and as large as the textured body stands now. «הסרת מרקם» gives the plain body back. Mask bodies count again when the texture is changed: hide them rather than delete them if you may change it later. Undo takes back each of these steps as well.

For a print in two colours, switch on «דוגמה כחלק נפרד», with any pattern: the pattern becomes a part of its own, and «צבע הדוגמה» gives it its colour – to begin with one that stands out against the body, the next time the one you chose last. Out of the body, the part is what stands out, and the body stays as it was; into the body, the body gets the pattern cut in and the part fills the cut up to the surface, like the gold of the cracks. Both go together in a bundle: they move together, each keeps its colour, and a 3MF has them as two parts for the slicer. Change or remove the texture as before; the pattern part goes along. With several layers, all have to go the same way, out of the body or into it; a pattern going both ways stays one with the body.

Painting in several colours

For a print in several colours, «צביעה» colours parts of a body right in the design: a face, a round wall, the inside of a hole, a round patch or an area you draw round. It works like the paint tools of the slicers, but the paint is saved with the design. Select one body and click «צביעה» in the «משטח» group, next to «מרקם משטח». The panel offers the colours of your design, and «צבע משלך» mixes one of your own. Then paint in the view with one of six tools:

  • «מילוי»: a click fills the face up to its sharp edges, like the bucket of a paint program: the top of a box, the wall of a cylinder, the floor of a pocket or the inside of a hole, each in one click. «עצירה בקצוות חדים מ-» says how sharp an edge has to be to stop the fill; at 90° it runs over the edges of a box.
  • «עיגול»: a click lays a round patch of the «קוטר» set onto the surface, centred where you click, on a curved surface too. It stops at the body's sharp edges and never comes through on the back of a wall.
  • «מברשת»: hold the mouse button or the pen down on the body and paint a line, «קוטר» wide. It follows the surface over curves and edges and never comes through on the back of a wall. With a pen or a drawing tablet, «לחץ העט קובע את הרוחב» makes a light touch paint thinner, down to a fifth of the diameter: good for eyes, teeth and fine lines. Ctrl and the mouse wheel, or the keys [ and ], make the brush or the circle larger or smaller; a see-through ball on the body shows where it paints. With «ככדור: גם קצוות מעוגלים» the circle and the brush paint everything inside the ball, round edges and into hollows too, as the slicers' sphere does.
  • «רצועת גובה»: a click paints a band «גובה הרצועה» tall level round the whole body at that height, like a headband; a see-through ring shows where it runs.
  • «לאסו»: hold the mouse button and draw round an area. What you see inside the shape is painted; the back of the body behind it is not.
  • «לאסו מגנטי»: click roughly along an edge, and the line finds it: where the surface bends, the body's outline against what lies behind it, or the border of paint already on it. Click the first point, double-click or press Enter to close it; Backspace takes the last point back, Esc throws the line away. What you see inside it is painted, as with the lasso: to fill an eye you painted round with the brush, click once round its border. It also feels the gentle bends of a modelled surface, such as the rim of an eye socket. «החלקה» evens the line out before it is laid, for the lasso too; corners stay where they are.

«מחק» takes the paint off again with the same tools, «ניקוי הכול» takes all of it off at once. Every stroke is a step of its own, so Ctrl+Z takes the last one back. Between strokes the right mouse button turns the view as always; «סיום» or Esc closes the panel. «מילוי פערים» tidies up: patches of one colour smaller than «כתמים עד» take the colour round them, so stray specks go and the printer changes colour less often.

The edges of a circle or a lasso are cut into the body's mesh, so a circle on the face of a box is round, not a few triangles, and the body stays closed. The paint belongs to the body: it moves and turns with it, stretches with it when you resize it, and a stroke that finds nothing any more after a change is left out. A 3MF export writes the paint as painted faces, which OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio and PrusaSlicer print with their filaments (see Files and saving); an OBJ keeps the colours too, an STL stays one colour.

A group cut into one body, a box with its holes for instance, is painted as one body: paint it after cutting the holes, and its paint stays when you change the group in an open group. The parts of a bundle are painted one by one. A painted body that you group with others keeps its paint as a part and shows it again when you ungroup it. An imported mesh that is not closed is painted on its own triangles.

A vase

The «אגרטל» is a body of its own for vases, pots and lampshades. Its measures are under «מאפיינים», its form and pattern on the card «צורה ודוגמה»; all of it can be changed at any time, also after saving:

  • «צורת הגוף»: bottle, bellied, bowl with neck, barrel, cylinder or funnel. The height and the widest diameter are the shape's own measures: drag the handles, or type «קוטר» and «גובה».
  • «חתך»: a circle, a polygon with rounded corners or a wave (soft, pointed, arches or fins). «פיתול (°)» turns it up the height, «נדנוד (°)» lets it swing back and forth.
  • «בנייה»: «מלא (מצב אגרטל)» is for the slicer's vase mode (also called spiralize): the slicer prints one wall in a spiral and leaves out the top and the infill, so the body is drawn solid. «חלול עם דופן» gives a real wall and bottom for a normal print. With «תחתית» at 0 mm the hollow vase has no bottom: a tube open at both ends, for bells, mouthpieces and pipes.
  • «דוגמה»: a new vase is plain. To choose from are grooves, stripes, Voronoi cells or facets (low poly) on the outside, in a band of the height if you like; they belong to the vase and stay editable with it.

«חתך למעלה» blends the cross-section into a second one up the height, say from a circle at the bottom to a hexagon at the top.

«ציור האגרטל», high up in the vase's settings, opens a large window of its own over the work area: in it you draw the vase yourself, in real millimetres. You move it by its title bar and make it larger from its corner; the vase in the view follows every change. «צורת הגוף» shows it from the side: drag the points, the top one sets the height, the widest one the diameter; «ידית» gives a point an angle, «פינה» a kink. «חתך» shows the cross-section from above, and the «סימטריה» mirrors or repeats what you draw. «פיתול» draws the twist as a curve up the height. An SVG file from a drawing program becomes the cross-section or the body form, the mesh of a model vase (STL, 3MF, OBJ) the body form. A photo of a vase can go behind the drawing to trace, or «לקיחת קו מתאר» takes its outline. «המרה לקווים» does the same with settings: «סף» for how clearly the object must stand out from the background, «נקודות» (3 to 40) and «פינה מ-»; the new body form shows in red over the photo first and becomes the vase's with «החלה». Where the vase stands before a busy background (a shelf, a room), the search does not find it; then the «לאסו מגנטי» catches it: click roughly round the vase, between two clicks the line follows the edge by itself, and a click on the first point, a double click or Enter closes it. The body form then comes from the lasso; «ביטול הלאסו» lets the search look again. One drag of the mouse is one step of undo; while you drag, the view shows the vase roughly.

The card «תחתית ודופן» holds what the body needs besides its form. On a hollow vase: drain holes with a slope towards them, a hollow foot, an open wall («דופן פתוחה»: Voronoi, honeycomb, diamonds or circles, for lanterns and baskets) and a «אהיל», hanging, standing on a socket or over an LED puck. On a solid vase, «מצב אגרטל חזק יותר» cuts fine slits through which the slicer prints ribs inside or a double wall in its vase mode, in one go. Recesses for rubber feet go with either. «חייב להחזיק מים» raises the wall and bottom of a hollow vase to at least 1.6 mm.

On the card «אביזרים» the vase gets its accessories. «רגליים» can be moulded on, so they are part of the vase, or made to plug in, with sockets in the bottom; a stand holds the vase from below. A «תחתית» catches the water, or hides in the foot. A hollow vase can take an «תותב»: a plant insert, one with a water store for a pot that waters itself, a holder for stems or water tubes. A «מכסה» slips over the rim, snaps into a groove or screws onto a thread in the wall. As a «מכסה קומקום» the top of the vase itself becomes the lid, as on a teapot: «גובה המכסה» (or «הגוף עד · המכסה מ-», from the bottom) sets how much, the pattern and the twist run on across the joint, a lip holds it in the opening, and «ידית (מודפסת בנפרד)» adds a knob that is glued into a hole on top. «סידור אביזרים» lays the parts out beside the vase as objects of their own, ready to print, upside down where that prints better. The vase already has the sockets, groove or thread. After changing a setting, lay them out again: the parts from before are replaced. If a part does not fit this vase, the editor says so, for example when an insert would not get through a narrow neck.

The card «הדפסה» tells what the vase takes to print: its weight in the chosen «חומר» and the length of filament – for a solid vase worked out for the slicer's vase mode, one line along the wall. Below are notes on printing it: an overhang of more than 45° and where it is, a wall that is too thin, what a lid, a lamp or legs need, and the slicer settings for ribs and a double wall. A teapot lid is checked as it prints, upside down on its top: there a shoulder that narrows towards the rim overhangs.

«פירוט» sets how finely the vase is divided into triangles: finer looks smoother, but makes the editor slower with large vases.
