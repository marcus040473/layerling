## Summary

- **Trace in the sketch:** the reference image's settings get **Trace**. It traces the outline of the shape in the picture – a silhouette, a logo, a word – and shows it in red over the picture: square points for corners, round ones for smooth points. Settings: **Dark shape / Light shape**, **Threshold**, **Points** (how many the outlines get together, from the fewest the shape allows to a close fit; the hint under it gives the count and the corners), **Corner from** (degrees; 180 makes none), **Leave out specks up to** (mm) and **Keep holes** (off: only the outermost outlines). **Apply** puts the outlines into the sketch where the picture lies – its size, turn and crop, only the part it shows – selected, one step to undo. They are ordinary sketch points: corners plain or split between curves, smooth points with their handles in line, so they move, curve and remove like drawn ones, and holes are outlines inside others as usual.
- **The object before its background:** under **Shape**, **Object** beside Dark shape and Light shape. The background is the colour at the picture's edge (the middle of its pixels' colours); all that is close to it and joined to the edge is background, everything else is one outline, whatever its colours – a toy photographed on white comes out as one clean outline instead of dozens round its logo, its highlights and its wheels. **Threshold** sets how far a colour must lie from the background's. A colourful picture on an even background starts this way, without holes; with **Keep holes**, the patches of background colour the object encloses are its holes.
- **Pick areas:** a click on the picture outlines the patch of like colour round the spot (**Colour tolerance**), as a hole – the window of a train, say. An area that reaches the picture's edge is the background and is not taken (the card says so).
- **Cut away:** clicks on the picture set the corners of a part to take from the shape before its outline is found, whatever its colour – a shadow under a toy as dark as the wheels above it, which no threshold or colour tells apart. A click on the first corner, a double click or Enter closes it, Escape throws the corners away; it shows dashed over the picture. Inside the shape a cut is a hole whatever the holes are; at its edge the outline runs along the cut; on the object it lies on the background.
- **Holes and areas:** each hole, clicked area and cut is listed; pointing at one shows it stronger in the preview, ✕ takes it out (holes taken out can be brought back), and with **Cut out** off it is drawn as a construction line instead of a hole. Holes are named by where they lie, so a choice outlasts a small change of the threshold; an island in a hole goes with its hole; an area clicked in a hole takes its place.
- **Trace in the vase drawing:** beside **Take outline**, the photo row gets **Trace**: the body form from the photo with **Threshold** (how far a colour lies from the background's), **Points** (3 to 40) and **Corner from**, shown dashed in red over the photo before **Apply** makes it the vase's. The photo follows the object as the threshold changes it; Cancel lays it back.
- **The tracer** (`pictureTrace.ts`) builds on `rasterTrace`'s outline (its field, softening and marching squares, now exported): the outline walked in even steps; a corner where the line turns by more than the corner angle within three pixels while it runs on straight either side (a tight curve turns as much, but spread out), placed where the two sides meet; between the corners cubic Béziers fitted by least squares and split where they stray (after Schneider, Graphics Gems 1990), as loosely as the number of points allows (a bisection on the tolerance). No new dependency.
- `findObject` takes a threshold and `profileFromSamples` a number of points and a corner angle; without them both work as before.
- Guide: two paragraphs in "A picture as template" (chapter 7) and a sentence in the vase drawing (chapter 3). Texts in English, German, French, Dutch, Russian, Czech, Slovenian and Danish.

## Notes

- **Storage:** nothing new. Trace only adds ordinary points and sides (construction lines for areas not cut out) to the sketch, or a body form to the vase.
- The idea came from the trace tools of laser programs (LightBurn and Inkscape trace with Potrace); this is written from scratch – Potrace's code is GPL – on the outline `rasterTrace` already finds.
- The picture is traced in the main thread, at most 1000 pixels along its longer side, again once a slider rests for 90 ms; a busy picture takes up to a few hundred ms per change.
- Polish misses the new sentences; the translations other than English and German are mine and worth a native reader.
- No CHANGELOG or whatsNew in the diff, so it stays free of conflicts with yours.
- Developed with the help of Claude Code.
