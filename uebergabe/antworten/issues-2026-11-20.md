## #1899 – Text verschiebt sich beim Namensschild im 3MF

Worum es geht: Ein in der Größe verändertes Namensschild wurde beim Export mit falsch großen Schichten geschrieben.
Das betraf auch STL, im 3MF fällt es nur stärker auf.

```
Thank you for the screenshots, they made it easy to find. A name tag is made of text layers. When you resize the tag, the view stretches those layers together, but the export built each layer again as a text of its own, and a text keeps its letters' proportions. So a tag pulled wider was written at its old width, and one made larger had each layer at a slightly different size. That is why the letters no longer sat on the plate in the slicer.

I have a fix ready: a resized name tag is now exported exactly as the view shows it. I will send it to Stefan as a pull request. Until then, a tag at its original size exports correctly, so setting the text size in the tag's own settings rather than pulling the handles avoids it.
```

## #1781 – Teile fehlen im 3MF

```
Thank you, and sorry it happened again. I could not open your attached file here, but I found one way small parts get lost only in the 3MF: when a small part such as a tooth is grouped and another body of a different colour covers most of it, the little bit that still sticks out was treated as a leftover splinter of a cut and removed. The STL of the same parts keeps them, which matches what you saw.

I have a fix ready for that and will send it to Stefan. To be sure it is the same thing in your design, could you tell me two things? Did the export show a notice that tiny loose pieces were left out? And how are the teeth made: are they grouped, and roughly how big are they?
```

## #1863 – „Error creating WebGL context“

Worum es geht: Kein Fehler in Layerling. Der Browser bekommt keine 3D-Grafik. Hilfestellung.

```
Sorry you are stuck. That message comes from the browser: it could not start 3D graphics, so the editor has nothing to draw with. A few things usually bring it back. Close other tabs and restart the browser, or the whole computer. In Chrome or Edge, check that "Use graphics acceleration when available" is switched on in Settings under System, then restart the browser. Updating the graphics driver helps on older machines. You can check at get.webgl.org whether 3D works at all in that browser. If it does not work there either, another browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) is worth a try. Which browser and computer are you using?
```
